Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to UptimePulse ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, process, and safeguard your data when using our website health, response latency, and SSL certificate expiration monitoring services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information necessary to deliver high-availability synthetic monitoring services:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, and encrypted (BCrypt hashed) passwords.
  • Monitored Target Data: Target website URLs, hostnames, port numbers, check intervals, and custom labels.
  • Telemetry & Audit Logs: HTTP status codes, socket latency response times (ms), SSL certificate expiry dates, and alert timestamps.
  • Alert Channel Integrations: Slack webhook URLs, Discord webhook URLs, and destination email addresses.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

All persistent data is securely stored within an isolated, enterprise-grade PostgreSQL relational database. Passwords are never stored in plaintext and are protected using industry-standard salted BCrypt password hashing. Communication between your browser and our servers is strictly encrypted via HTTPS / TLS.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use strictly necessary browser cookies and localStorage solely for maintaining your authenticated JWT session state and remembering your cookie consent preferences. We do not sell your personal data or utilize intrusive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

5. Third-Party Integrations

When you configure external alert notification channels (such as Slack incoming webhooks or Discord channel embeds), alert payloads are transmitted over outbound TLS connections directly to those services. We do not share your private metrics with unauthorized third parties.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently delete your account and associated monitoring targets. To exercise these rights, you may manage your monitors directly via the dashboard or submit a data deletion request.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, inquiries, or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact our security and compliance team at privacy@uptimepulse.com.
